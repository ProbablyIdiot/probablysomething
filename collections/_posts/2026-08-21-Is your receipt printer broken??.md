Why does HMV need my email address??

On Wednesday I decided to cycle from where I live (Worcester) to where I used to live (Birmingham) to meet up with my mate and grab lunch together.

| ![My route from Worcester to Birmingham along the canal (start and end anonymised for obvious reasons)](/assets/images/blogImgs/210826-cycleRoute.png) |
| *My route from Worcester to Birmingham along the canal (start and end anonymised for obvious reasons)* |

After arriving at his house, we walked to the train station and went to get lunch at Tortilla in Grand Central. After this we decided to do our usual activity of messing about in the shops in town. 

As part of this, we visited the HMV Bullring shop. I found a great deal on Hickey by Royel Otis, which was discounted to just £8.99, so I grabbed a copy for myself. (They also had NOW! albums for just £3.99, but after looking at the tracklist, it was obvious why no one was buying them)

After making fun of some of the products in the shop, I headed to the checkout to pay for my album. It went as usual, I handed over the CD, the man scanned it, then I paid with my card as I didn't have enough cash in my wallet. However, as I went to grab my CD from the counter, the man span the screen round and asked for my email address. **Apparently HMV only does e-receipts via email now.** Regardless of the fact that there was a receipt printer clearly visible on the counter, I had to clumsily enter my email into the touchscreen and opt out of marketing emails before I could stuff the CD into my bag and leave the shop.

**Why can't I just get a paper receipt or decline one entirely?** It's not like I had any use for the electronic one either, it added nothing to my experience, except now my email is in yet another database ready to be leaked in a future data breach (I really should've used my throwaway email for this, but oh well). But no, I must now hand over my email even if I were paying for physical media with cash. 

Come on HMV, I quite liked you as a brand, but this really doesn't make me feel better about shopping there...
