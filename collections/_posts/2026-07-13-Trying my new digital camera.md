I went for a bike ride to test out my new digital camera today, these are my thoughts about using it.

![A photo of a Panasonic Lumix DMC-FX3 Camera](/assets/images/blogImgs/120726-lumix0.jpg)

I'm going interrailing in the summer and want to take a standalone camera with me to reduce the amount I need to have my phone out while still being able to take pictures. Originally I was going to use the old family camera (y'know because we already had it), however when I eventually dug it out i found it was in quite a dire state. After me and my brother were let loose with it when we were younger, the camera is quite beaten up, with the shutter now not even closing when the camera is not in use. For this reason, and it's use of xD cards for storage instead of SD cards, I started looking for a new camera.

| ![A photo of the old, battered, family camera (an Olympus ų740)](/assets/images/blogImgs/120726-olympus.jpg) |
| *The battered family camera (an Olympus ų740)* |

I had been searching on eBay for a new camera and found some good deals on autions (3 for £7, etc). I put a bid on a few of these, however every time the price would jump from ~£15 an hour before the end, to around SEVENTY POUNDS! For this reason, I stopped trying to bid on auctions, and just kept an eye out for a good buy it now deal. During this time, however, I found the panasonic camera in the back of a cupboard at the school where I'm doing work experience. I had a quick chat with the head of IT and he said I could keep it. This particular camera (the Panasonic Lumix DMC-FX3) is actually a slight downgrade from the camera we already had - 6 megapixels instead of the 7.1 on the Olympus. However, the fact that I had the PC cable, took SD cards, and that it wasn't beaten up makes it a better choice (even though the pictures are slightly worse).

| ![A photo through Worcester viaduct towards All Saints Church](/assets/images/blogImgs/260713_lumixPhoto0.JPG)
| *Through Worcester viaduct towards All Saints Church* |

| ![A photo of Worcester Racecourse during sunset](/assets/images/blogImgs/260713_lumixPhoto1.JPG) |

These photos show how the camera handles low-light and high contrast situations. I had to edit the photo through the viaduct after the fact, as the ISO I had selected for this shot was too low (hard to tell on the crappy camera LCD). This shows how the camera can have quite bad blotchiness in darker areas. However, I like the photos the camera creates, as I think they have a nice effect to them. They have enough detail to accurately recreate details in the picture, however they still have that memory-like 2000s look (oooh look I'm being #aesthetic - the girls I know would be proud). 

I'll pop a few extra pictures in underneath from my bike ride beneath here, however I'm not the greatest photographer, so don't expect anything amazing.
