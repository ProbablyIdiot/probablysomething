**Parachutes is perhaps the best album the band has released.** 

While some songs (looking at you, We Never Change) aren't the band's greatest work, no song in the album is truly un-listenable. For a debut studio album (we don't talk about Ode to Deodorant, which gives Yuno Miles a run for his money) it is a very good showcase of just what the band is capable of.

Don't panic is an undeniably good opening song, and if it wasn't for how short it is, it would undeniably be my favourite Coldplay song. The rest of the album is full of unique and likeable songs - Spies in particular feels like a style of song we haven't seen since from the band. I feel like they lean much more heavily into more "traditional" instruments for this song (drums, guitar, etc), rather than going for synth and voice effects, which nicely places more of a focus onto the deep and meaningful lyrics offered by many of the songs. Some songs such as We Never Change and High speed definitely detract from the listening experience, but not to the extent of something like Pyramid from Two Door Cinema Club's otherwise outstanding album Beacon.

Overall, this album has an authentic, genuine feel to it which the band seems to have lost over recent years. It proves that you don't need an astronomic, moon-sized budget to produce good music. The lack of collaborations isn't what makes a good album, but too many can certainly detract from it (cough, cough Moon Music). While the entire album (Yellow in particular) suffers from the problem of people saying it is "the greatest thing they've ever heard", it's a great listen and an album I don't stop coming back to.
